How to play
  • Type a word that changes exactly one letter of the word above it, then press Enter or Play word.
  • Every step must be a real 5-letter word. Going back to an earlier word is allowed, but it counts as a step.
  • Green boxes are letters already in their DANTE spot. Blue marks the letter changed on that step.
  • Par is the fewest steps possible. Everyone gets the same word, and a new one arrives at midnight Eastern time.
  • The last step is always DANCE to DANTE, and DANCE sits in a small corner of the dictionary. Rare words like HANCE (part of an arch), RANCE (a red Belgian marble) and BUNCE (British slang for a windfall) are the way in.
